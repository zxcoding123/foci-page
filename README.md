# 🎧 FoCi

> **Find your flow in the depth of sound.**

FoCi is a minimalist productivity environment engineered to shield your attention from external noise. Built on deep-frequency soundscapes—brown noise, subterranean drones, and rhythmic acoustic pulses—FoCi acts as a cognitive anchor for deep work, coding, and heavy writing sessions.

---

## 🎯 The Philosophy

Most productivity apps try to **manage your time**. FoCi is built to **protect it**. 

Traditional lo-fi playlists often introduce dynamic shifts, vocals, or melodic changes that pull your brain out of focus. FoCi utilizes consistent, low-end frequencies to:

* 🛡️ **Mask Environmental Noise:** Neutralize office chatter, traffic, and high-frequency household sounds.
* 🧠 **Lower Cognitive Fatigue:** Remove melodic unpredictability to let your brain settle into uninterrupted execution.
* 🌊 **Accelerate Flow States:** Provide a non-intrusive auditory foundation that helps you enter and maintain deep concentration.

---

## 🛠️ Key Features

### 🔊 Curated Sound Engine
An ever-growing library of high-fidelity, seamless audio loops designed specifically for sustained focus:

| Profile | Best For | Acoustic Profile |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Deep Brown Noise** | Heavy masking & calm | Warm, rich low-end frequency roll-off |
| **Subterranean Hum** | Coding & intense logic | Low-frequency mechanical drone |
| **Atmospheric Textures** | Creative writing & design | Organic, non-melodic layers (distant rain, heavy wind) |

### 🎚️ Minimalist Mixer
* **Custom Stacking:** Layer up to 4 sound profiles simultaneously.
* **Focus Presets:** Save and instantly load tailored acoustic mixes for different tasks (e.g., *Writing*, *Debugging*, *Reading*).

### ⏱️ Deep Work Timer
* **Gentle Transitions:** No jarring alarms. Sounds slowly fade out to signal rest breaks and fade back in for focus blocks.
* **Unobtrusive HUD:** Stays out of your line of sight until you need it.

### 🎨 Intentional Design
* **Zero Distraction:** No feeds, no ads, no complex setup.
* **OLED Dark-Mode First:** Restful on the eyes during late-night build sessions.
* **Fluid UI:** Minimalist micro-interactions designed to match the calm tone of the audio.
